# Digital agency creative direction

## Ambition
Demonstrate the agency's ability before the first conversation. Combine memorable typography, original composition, and restrained motion with honest evidence and a clear project path.

## What this replaces
The previous local idea was a finance application. This repository starts from scratch. The agency needs its own positioning, portfolio, service story, studio identity, and commissioning journey.

## Experience
1. Arrival: oversized type, concise positioning, and an interactive original visual.
2. Work: three visually contrasting concept studies, explicitly labelled.
3. Point of view: a strong typographic interlude explaining the agency ambition.
4. Expertise: expandable disciplines with outcomes and deliverables.
5. Process: discovery, definition, design, and delivery.
6. Invitation: shape a project brief with ambition, investment, and timing.

## Art direction
Warm paper, deep green-black ink, and a vivid chartreuse accent. Manrope and DM Sans support editorial scale, with serif italics adding contrast. Terrain, Ondo, and Folio demonstrate three distinct proposed visual worlds using original CSS compositions.

Motion must serve hierarchy and interaction. Scroll remains native. Text is present before animation, reduced-motion preference disables reveals and looping movement, and essential controls work with touch and keyboard.

## Content integrity
FORME is a working name. The portfolio is self-initiated concept work, not a client list. No testimonials, awards, team biographies, conversion results, or business metrics have been fabricated. Capabilities are marked as proposed.

## Conversion
The first implementation creates a downloadable brief entirely in the visitor's browser. It cannot receive enquiries until a real destination is supplied. This constraint is stated beside the form and in its completion message.

## Next decisions
Confirm the agency name, available portfolio material, real service scope, contact destination, commercial budget bands, and preferred hosting/domain. These are launch inputs; the first visual implementation is reviewable without them.
