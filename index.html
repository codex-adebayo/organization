FORME is a working identity for an independent digital agency. The ambition is simple: bring thoughtful strategy, distinctive design, and considered engineering into the same conversation.

Because a website should do more than look good. It should express who you are, make your value clear, and give people a reason to take the next step.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the ambition.

We listen, challenge assumptions, and define what success should look like. You bring the context; we shape the right questions.

Output: a shared brief and clear priorities.
02 / DEFINE

Find the central idea.

We connect the positioning, story, and experience into a direction that feels specific to your business.

Output: creative direction and experience structure.
03 / DESIGN

Make it feel inevitable.

We explore, prototype, and refine the visual system with you. Typography, content, and motion develop together.

Output: responsive designs and an interactive prototype.
04 / DELIVER

We build, test, and prepare for launch, checking the experience across screens, inputs, and motion preferences.

Output: a tested website and a practical handover.
